Drupal 11.4+ [comes with a Symfony Console application called Doctor](https://www.drupal.org/node/3584928) 🩺. You run it by calling `vendor/bin/dr`. Doctor is a small but mighty application, offering a few commands like `cron` and `install`. I have been [contributing](https://www.drupal.org/project/drupal/issues/3627514) [commands](https://www.drupal.org/project/drupal/issues/3594333) to Doctor - alas the core review and merge process can be slow. Many vital commands are not in Drupal core yet. So, Drush is throwing its full weight behind Doctor and [making nearly all of its commands available](https://github.com/drush-ops/drush/blob/15.x/src/Doctor/DoctorServiceProvider.php) to `dr`. After you `composer require drush/drush ^15` into your project, you can call `dr deploy`, `dr updatedb`, and most of your Drush friends.

![dr list on Drupal 12 with Drush 15]({{ "/assets/drush-15/dr-list.png" | relative_url }} "dr list on Drupal 12 with Drush 15 installed")

### What's gone

Drush 15 ([docs](https://www.drush.org/15.x/)) only provides commands to `dr`. It no longer has:

- Site aliases. Drush doesn't run commands on remote hosts.
- Config files (`drush.yml`).
- The `drush` entrypoint. Call Drupal's `dr` instead.
- Discovery of Drush-style commands in custom and contrib modules.

Drush's [seppuku](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seppuku) makes Doctor the sole focus of Drupal 12 command porting efforts.

The Drush 14.x branch is unsupported and will not be released. It is available for sites that really want to "pre-upgrade" before they move to Drupal 12. Drush 14.x still does discovery of contrib/custom commands. Once the upgrade is complete, please upgrade to Drush 15 to regain support.

### Porting your commands

Use [these](https://www.drush.org/15.x/commands/#upgrading-from-drush-13) [docs](https://www.drupal.org/docs/develop/drupal-apis/command-line-interface-cli-api/drush-command-porting-guide-to-the-new-dr-drupal-core-cli), to port a core or contrib Drush command. Or feed this prompt to your agent: *Port this Drush command to a plain Symfony Console command that Drupal's `dr` loads.* Once ported, your command works on Drupal 11.4+ and Drupal 12. If you are porting a contrib module (as opposed to custom), be sure to share your port with the project to speed up everyone else's Drupal upgrade.

### Helping Doctor

You can help Doctor by reviewing [issues in the CLI subsystem](https://www.drupal.org/project/issues/drupal?text=&status=Open&priorities=All&categories=All&version=All&component=CLI+system). Please review generously and if you call for a change, create/update the MR at the same time. Contrary to popular belief, this does not make you ineligible to mark the issue RTBC. The spirit of that guideline is that more than one person should approve of a change before it is marked RTBC. Your review is that second person.

If you have thoughts about this effort, lets discuss in #drush on Drupal Slack, or in the [Drush issue queue](https://github.com/drush-ops/drush/issues).

We are pretty far along in [Drush's final act](https://weitzman.github.io/blog/drush-final-act). It's a gratifying feeling for Drupal core to finally gain a full featured CLI.
